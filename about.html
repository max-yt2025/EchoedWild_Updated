About EchoedWild

EchoedWild is a comprehensive wildlife education platform dedicated to raising awareness about endangered species and promoting conservation efforts worldwide. Our mission is to provide students, educators, and wildlife enthusiasts with accurate, engaging, and accessible information about the world's most vulnerable animals.

At EchoedWild, we believe that knowledge is the first step toward conservation. By understanding the challenges facing endangered species, the importance of biodiversity, and the interconnectedness of all living things, we can all become better stewards of our planet. Our platform features detailed profiles of over 100 animal species, ranging from the majestic African elephant to the elusive vaquita porpoise.

EchoedWild exists because wildlife education matters more than ever. With habitat loss, climate change, and human activities threatening countless species, there's an urgent need for accessible, reliable information that can inspire action. Whether you're a student working on a research project, a teacher looking for classroom resources, or simply someone who cares about wildlife, EchoedWild provides the tools and knowledge you need to make a difference.

Our Mission

Our educational mission extends beyond simple facts and figures. We aim to foster a deeper understanding of ecosystems, conservation science, and the complex relationships between species and their habitats. Through our status tracker, you can monitor the conservation status of endangered species in real-time, staying informed about the latest updates from conservation organizations worldwide.

Wildlife conservation is a collective responsibility. By educating ourselves and others, supporting conservation organizations, and making conscious choices in our daily lives, we can all contribute to protecting the incredible diversity of life on Earth. EchoedWild is here to guide you on that journey, providing the knowledge and resources you need to become an effective advocate for wildlife.

What We Offer

100+ Animal Profiles

Comprehensive profiles of endangered and threatened species, including detailed information about habitat, behavior, diet, and conservation status.

Conservation Status Tracker

Real-time tracking of species conservation status using IUCN Red List categories, helping you understand which species are most at risk.

Educational Resources

Curated content designed for students, teachers, and lifelong learners, aligned with educational standards and best practices.

Conservation Action Guide

Practical steps you can take to help protect wildlife, from reducing plastic use to supporting conservation organizations.

Our Values

Accuracy

We prioritize scientific accuracy and reliability, sourcing information from reputable conservation organizations and scientific publications.

Accessibility

Wildlife education should be accessible to everyone. Our content is designed to be clear, engaging, and available to users of all backgrounds.

Action-Oriented

We don't just provide information—we inspire action. Every page includes practical ways you can contribute to wildlife conservation.

Empathy

We approach wildlife education with empathy and respect, recognizing the intrinsic value of all living beings and ecosystems.
