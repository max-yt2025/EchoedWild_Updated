Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2025

At EchoedWild, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our wildlife education platform. We are committed to transparency and ensuring that your data is handled responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

EchoedWild collects minimal personal data to provide and improve our educational services. We may collect the following types of information:

Information You Provide

  • Email Address: When you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us via our contact form
  • Contact Messages: Content of messages sent through our contact form
  • Feedback: Any voluntary feedback, suggestions, or comments you provide

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns
  • IP Address: Anonymized IP address for security and analytics purposes
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device for functionality and preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide and maintain our educational platform and services
  • Send newsletters and educational content to subscribers
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Third-Party Services

EchoedWild uses third-party services to enhance functionality and user experience. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies:

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to display typography. Google may collect IP address and font usage data. Please review Google's privacy policy for details.

Music Widgets

We embed music using third-party services that may track play counts, device type, or general location data. EchoedWild does not store or access this data directly.

Chatbot Services

Our chatbot is powered by third-party providers. Conversations may be logged or analyzed to improve functionality. EchoedWild does not store or access these conversation logs.

Email Subscription (Buttondown)

Newsletter subscriptions are managed through Buttondown, which securely stores your email address. We do not share or sell your email information. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Contact Form (Formspree)

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree. Messages are used only to respond to your inquiries and are not stored or shared for other purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and analyze site performance:

Essential Cookies

Required for basic platform functionality, including navigation and access to secure areas.

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings and preferences, such as cookie consent choices and display preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our platform to improve user experience and content relevance.

You can accept or decline cookies through our popup notification. Your choice is stored locally on your device and does not impact your ability to use core platform features.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure storage of email addresses and contact information
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Access controls and authentication for administrative functions
  • Compliance with data protection best practices

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Email Addresses: Retained until you unsubscribe from our newsletter
  • Contact Messages: Retained as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry
  • Usage Data: Retained for analysis and platform improvement purposes
  • Cookies: Retained according to your browser settings until deleted

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
    • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Object: Object to processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Complaint: File a complaint with relevant data protection authorities

8. Children's Privacy

EchoedWild is an educational platform that may be used by students and minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. When we transfer your information internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your privacy in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes by posting the new policy on our platform and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of EchoedWild after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Custom UI Elements

Our site includes custom cursors, scrollbars, and animations for aesthetic purposes. These client-side elements do not collect or store any user data and are implemented purely for visual enhancement and user experience.

12. Analytics

We currently use minimal analytics to understand platform usage and improve user experience. If we implement additional analytics services in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect what data is collected and how it is used. We are committed to using analytics responsibly and in accordance with privacy best practices.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise your privacy rights, or have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us through our contact form. We will respond to your inquiry within a reasonable timeframe.
